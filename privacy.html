Privacy Policy

How XDock protects and respects your privacy

1 No Personal Information Collected

XDock respects your privacy above all else. You can access and utilize all features of XDock entirely anonymously. We do not require registration, user accounts, personal names, phone numbers, or passwords.

2 Zero Media Storage & No Video Logging

  • No Storage: XDock does not host, store, or archive downloaded videos or audio files on any servers.
  • Direct Stream: All video download requests are processed in real-time and streamed directly from Twitter's official Content Delivery Networks (CDNs) to your device.
  • No Download History: We do not keep logs or records of the videos you download or the URLs you submit.

3 Technical & Anonymous Log Data

Like standard web services, our web server may record non-personal technical data (such as browser type, device operating system, referring URL, and approximate timestamp) solely for load balancing, network optimization, and preventing automated DDoS attacks. This data is strictly anonymous and cannot identify any user individually.

4 Cookies & Local Storage

Our website uses only essential session data and cookies strictly required to ensure stable site performance and user preferences. We do not use intrusive cross-site tracking cookies to profile our visitors.

5 Third-Party Links & Disclaimer

XDock is an independent tool and is not affiliated, endorsed, or partnered with X Corp. or Twitter. External content and media are governed solely by Twitter's own terms and policies.

6 Questions & Website Info

For more information, project updates, or inquiries, visit the official website at napa-extraa.vercel.app or reach out through our Contact Form.
